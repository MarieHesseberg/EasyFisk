# Imagegen-spesifikasjoner

Verktøy: innebygd image_gen. Formål: stilforslag, ikke implementering.

## Felles innhold

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high fidelity professional design proposal IMAGE for the existing Norwegian mobile fishing app easyfisk. This is a preview concept, not production implementation. Landscape canvas about 1800x1200. Two equally sized large flat mobile app screens side by side, readable, each roughly 390x844 logical px, thin subtle device outline but NO photographic phone, NO perspective, NO browser UI. Board title at upper left and small palette swatches at lower left. Screens fill most of board; high quality actual mobile UI, not a wireframe. All typography sans-serif, approximately 17px body, 28px page title, 14px nav labels; generous but efficient whitespace, large touch areas at least 48px, readable dark secondary text. Short labels must be spelled correctly in Norwegian. Use consistent thin outline icons, no plus signs. Don't decorate everything with cards or big borders. Only one strong filled primary action per screen. Text never over photos. Avoid gradients and gratuitous shadows. Bottom navigation on both screens: icons and labels "Hjem", "Kart", "Fiskekort", "Regler", "Mer"; active Hjem left screen, Fiskekort right screen, active indication by shape/weight as well as color. Header on home: small fish-outline logo and "easyfisk", compact "EN" language control, bell icon.
LEFT SCREEN HOME exact content and hierarchy: page title "Din fiskeoversikt"; context "Mandalselva · Sone 3"; prominent filled full-width "Kjøp fiskekort" button. Immediately below an easy-to-find generous row "Mine fiskekort" with a ticket icon, secondary text "1 kommende kort", right chevron. Section heading "Før fisket"; explanatory line "Registrer dokumentene før du starter."; two secondary list rows "Desinfisering" and "Statlig fiskeravgift", each with readable "Registrer" action and appropriate outline icon. Lower section two quieter list rows "Registrer tidligere fisketur" with clock icon and "Meld fra til elveeigarlaget" with report icon, both chevrons. Keep all content above bottom navigation with sensible spacing. No yellow warning decoration on ordinary actions.
RIGHT SCREEN PURCHASE exact content: back chevron and "Kjøp fiskekort"; section "Hvor vil du fiske?" and labeled dropdown "Sone" displaying "Sone 3". Product "Døgnkort" and price "455 kr per dag". Heading "Velg fiskedager", body "Du kan velge flere enkeltdager." A compact August 2026 date selector with a row of seven LARGE tappable dates 20,21,22,23,24,25,26 (no weekday labels required); selected 20 and 22 visibly filled with a checkmark. Text "2 dager valgt". Section "Hvem skal fiske?" with selection "Meg selv" selected and "Kjøp til noen andre" unselected on separate clear rows. Small inline note "Navn og kontaktinfo hentes fra profilen din." Quiet order summary "2 døgnkort" left "910 kr" right. Full-width filled primary action "Fortsett". Same visual language as left screen, no extra panels. Both screens realistic, coherent, aesthetically polished, simple and accessible.

## A · Klar elveblå

Clean calm contemporary Nordic utility. Neutral near-white #F7F9FA canvas, white surfaces, ink #172C36, readable secondary #52616A, strong petrol blue #155E75 as the sole action accent. Typography inspired by Inter, regular body and medium labels, semibold only page/section headings. Flat primarily borderless grouped list rows with subtle separators, no big enclosing status card. Rounded 12px buttons and 16px one restrained owned-permit card, understated tint #EAF3F6 for that row only. Deliberate 8/16/24px spacing system. A refined precise functional app feeling, lots of visual calm. Board backdrop cool pale gray.

## B · Nordisk natur

Understated premium outdoors visual language. Warm white #FAFAF6 canvas, deep forest #205B48 primary action, near-black green #1E2C25 text, readable muted #56645D. Very subtle sage #EAF0E8 accents. Typography inspired by humanist Source Sans 3, friendly open letterforms, regular body and medium emphasis. Header has tiny abstract flowing river line, no photos. Open sections on warm white, divider rows rather than boxes, just one soft sage owned-permit tile. Rounded 18px primary buttons, polished organic but minimal, never rustic or beige/yellow. The SAME content and large text as the other variants. Board backdrop pale sage.

## C · Presis og lys

Very clean graphic Scandinavian / native iOS-inspired service app. White #FFFFFF background, charcoal #20242C typography, restrained clear royal blue #234DC3 only for key actions, neutral gray #F2F4F7 grouped list surfaces. Native system sans typography (SF Pro-like), 28px medium heading, 17px regular body, clear typographic contrast without heavy bold. Squarer 10px primary buttons, 12px grouped surfaces, fine neutral dividers. Flat, high clarity, very few colored regions, compact coherent top bar. Distinct from the softer blue and green concepts through sharper geometry and white/graphite/royal blue palette. No gradients/glass, no photos. Board backdrop light neutral gray.

## Justering av C

Bevar skjerminnholdet. Erstatt mørk bakgrunn utenfor skjermene med ensfarget #F2F4F7, og gjør tittelen og palettetikettene mørke og lesbare.
